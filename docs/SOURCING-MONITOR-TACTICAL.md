# Sourcing monitor: cumulative search progress

The landing table has one cumulative row per search across teams, with search/client filtering, a team filter and needs-attention filter. Every data column sorts the complete set. Click the search name, the chart icon or double-click its row for daily detail. Browser navigation preserves search and period selection. There is no pipeline view.

Targets sum team/day allocation targets through today, including today but excluding future days. No individual target is invented. Throughput means current approved profiles divided by that target. Quality means current approved profiles divided by mapped profiles. Pending decisions make these ratios provisional; wholly pending cohorts display Pending. Approval outcomes are attributed to the original mapping creation date in US Eastern time, with work_date as fallback. A later approval changes that earlier mapping cohort. Unknown mapping dates remain in cumulative totals, excluded from daily periods/charts.

Daily detail includes every date with allocations, estimated effort or mappings, including planned days without output. Select a day to see researcher effort, mapping and approval contribution shares, quality and pending profiles; click counts to inspect candidates. There is also a whole-period researcher table. No fabricated session counts are shown: effort comes from existing allocation/PTO/correction data using configured hours per day (default eight), not actual time logs.

Charts show cumulative approvals versus dated targets, daily mappings and eventual approvals, and daily approval ratio. Chart points link to daily researcher detail. Values reflect decisions known now, not historical approval snapshots. The cumulative chart includes earlier dates outside a custom window. Undated mappings cannot be positioned in the charts.

Attention uses the proposed inspection thresholds: approval ratio below 40% (minimum five mappings aged three days with no remaining undecided profiles in that mature cohort), estimated effort at least 200 hours, maps per active researcher-day below ten after at least three researcher-days, search age over 100 days, reviews pending over three days, effort with no mappings, missing planned targets, and matured target shortfalls. Target shortfalls allow three days for review and suppress delivery blame while mature decisions remain pending. Long duration does not infer client disinterest. Per-person low mapping pace compares mappings per estimated hour to half the peers' pace in the same search/period; inspect complexity and allocation before drawing conclusions. Ratio colors: green quality at least 70%, red below 40%, amber between; throughput green at target. Pending ratios are amber. These are review cues, not employee ratings.

Maps per person-day uses distinct dated researcher pairs with effort or mappings. Unattributed/undated mappings are excluded from the numerator. Shares and absolute counts retain unattributed historical profiles separately. Effort is split across all searches before team/search filtering and preserves PTO and recorded corrections.

Verification: synthetic tests cover Eastern date boundaries, late approvals assigned to the mapping day, future targets, researcher reconciliation, contribution shares, planned days without output, missing dates, review-lag flags, filtering, PTO and effort corrections. Typecheck, complete tests and build run before release; deployment and visual verification are recorded separately.
